      monochrome
      A colorful stand-in for the real app. On your phone the launcher just hands off to it.
      monochrome mode

      Color influences how you interact with your apps and can extend the time you spend on your phone.

      This is especially true for highly engaging apps like social media. Viewing them in monochrome can help you cut your screen time.

      Once it's on, you can turn monochrome on or off for each app.

      My minimal launcher

      A clickable mockup of the home screen we'll build for the Redmi 14C. Your changes in its settings are remembered in this browser.

      Try it

      • Swipe left (or click "swipe left for all apps") to open the app list. Swipe right to go back. Type to search, Enter opens the top result.
      • Long-press (or right-click) any app: favorite, rename, hide, or mark it distracting.
      • Launcher settings: the gear icon next to search in the app list, or long-press empty space on the home screen.
      • Open Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube to see the mindful pause.
      • Turn on Monochrome in settings, then open Instagram or TikTok: it shows up in grayscale. Long-press any app to change it.
      • Bottom buttons:recent apps (reopens your last app), home (or Esc), and back.

      v1 The core launcher build first, in the mockup

      • Text-only home screen: clock, date, battery, favorite apps
      • Full app list with instant search
      • Long-press menu: favorite, rename, hide
      • Settings: dark/light, text size, alignment, 12/24h clock
      • Registers as an Android home app so you can set it as default

      v2 Focus features in the mockup

      • Mindful pause countdown before distracting apps
      • Monochrome mode per app (on the real phone: a one-time, free setup step from your PC)
      • Phone and camera shortcuts in the corners
      • Gestures: swipe down for notifications, double-tap to lock

      v3 Later, harder not mocked yet

      • Screen time per app, plus daily limits
      • Notification filter: hold back notifications from noisy apps
      • Plain-text widgets: next calendar event, weather
